S2G Session Firewall Setup Guide

Secure your browser traffic with zero-trust session encryption.

1. Start the Proxy Server

1

Launch the **S2G Proxy Dashboard** application on your machine.

2

Click "Start Security Proxy" in the dashboard. Ensure it says "Listening on 127.0.0.1:8080".

2. Trust the Security Certificate

Because the proxy encrypts deep traffic, you must tell your browser to trust its local certificate.

1

Open this extension popup and toggle the firewall **ON**.

2

Navigate to mitm.it in a new tab.

3

Download the Windows certificate and install it to your **Trusted Root Certification Authorities** store.

4

Restart your browser. You are now fully protected!
